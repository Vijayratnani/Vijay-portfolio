## The problem

Voice conversations feel broken when replies take ~2s. Callers start talking over the bot, and the conversation falls apart.

## Architecture

The core change: **stream at every hop instead of waiting for whole turns.** Speech is transcribed as it arrives, a fast inference provider generates the reply, and synthesized speech streams back as it is produced.

On top of the pipeline, the agent handles:

- **Interruptions (barge-in):** the caller can talk over the bot.
- **Intent classification** to work out what the caller wants.
- **Multi-stage conversations** across several turns.

## Key decisions

- **A provider per stage, not one vendor:** Deepgram for speech-to-text, Groq or OpenAI for reasoning, and ElevenLabs for speech.
- **Groq for fast inference** on the reasoning step, where latency counts on every turn.
